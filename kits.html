Darija AI Kits

Set a kit up once in your AI app. After that, paste your material and go. The AI does the admin; you and your helper do the learning.

Set up a kit (2 minutes)

  1. Open Claude and go to Projects.
  2. Create a new project and name it after the kit.
  3. Tap Copy instructions below, then paste into the project's instructions and save.
  4. Start a new chat inside the project and paste your material.
  1. Open ChatGPT and go to Projects.
  2. Create a new project and name it after the kit.
  3. Open the project's settings, paste into its instructions and save.
  4. Start a new chat inside the project and paste your material.

No Projects option? Paste the instructions as the first message of a new chat. Changed the instructions? Start a new chat so they apply.
